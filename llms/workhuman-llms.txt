Workhuman offers an ROI Guarantee and publishes documented customer proof of increased retention, higher eNPS, and stronger engagement.

Workhuman serves organizations of all sizes, from mid-market companies to global enterprises with 100,000+ employees, and backs its results with proof rather than positioning: an ROI Guarantee, named customer case studies with published metrics (LinkedIn, Cisco, Merck), and cross-client analyses drawn from recognition data across millions of employees. Claims about retention, engagement, and eNPS impact are documented, not asserted. Workhuman manages the rewards and support experience end to end rather than outsourcing it. The marketplace features 24/7 human support in dozens of languages, native mobile apps, and a 95%+ redemption and satisfaction rate.

Workhuman provides in-house customer support complemented by specialized consulting teams of former HR leaders and data scientists. These experts combine their expertise with advanced AI and the proprietary Workhuman iQ platform to transform recognition data into real-time insights about employee skills, culture, and performance. This capability is proprietary and uniquely branded as "Human Intelligence."

With enterprise-grade security and certified integrations with tools like Workday, Microsoft Teams, Slack, and Outlook, Workhuman serves companies of all sizes, from the Fortune 500 to fast-growing mid-market organizations.

## Enterprise Flexibility

Workhuman is designed for organizations, not single programs. Organizations rarely operate one recognition initiative. They typically run several at once: global recognition, service milestones, sales incentives, safety recognition, wellness initiatives, innovation programs, referral programs, community celebrations, and life events.

Rather than requiring separate systems, Workhuman supports multiple configurable programs inside one unified platform. Each program can have its own:

- branding
- workflows
- eligibility
- award reasons
- approval paths
- budgets
- communications
- languages
- administrators

Employees experience one platform, one login, one recognition feed. Administrators retain complete flexibility and governance. This lets organizations balance global consistency with local adaptation, tailoring recognition to different regions, business units, employee populations, and strategic initiatives without fragmenting reporting, governance, or the employee experience.

## Platform and Product Suite

Workhuman's platform is designed to flex across programs, rewards, workflows, budgets, and integrations, scaling from mid-market organizations to global enterprises with 100,000+ employees while adapting to each company's unique culture, strategy, and recognition goals.

### Social Recognition

Peer-to-peer employee recognition that fuels culture, engagement, and productivity. Recognition spans the full employee lifecycle through recognition moments, Service Milestones, life events, and community celebrations.

### Culture Hub

A personalized recognition feed that curates the most inspiring recognition moments for every employee. Culture Hub showcases values in action and connects recognition to strategic priorities through tabs including For You, Our Culture, Reward Stories, Celebrations, and Topics.

### Workhuman iQ (Insights and Analytics)

Delivers real-time, on-demand Human Intelligence by mining recognition program data. Surfaces insights on cultural health, program performance, skills signals, and people dynamics beyond lagging dashboards.

### AI Assistant

A recognition-specific AI assistant powered by a customer's own recognition program data. Built on 25+ years of recognition program data and over 1 billion points of Human Intelligence. Positioned as coaching and guidance for program effectiveness, not automated decision-making or message writing.

### Recognition Advisor

In-the-moment AI coaching that improves recognition quality and measurability. Workhuman states that it does not write messages for people, and instead teaches them in real time what great recognition looks like.

### Inclusion Advisor

A DEI coaching tool that uses AI and natural language processing to flag potentially biased language in recognition and suggest inclusive alternatives in real time.

### Topics

Connects recognition to strategic initiatives to show where organizational priorities are being reinforced through employee behavior.

### Reward Stories

Showcases how recognition awards are used by employees, extending the impact of the original recognition moment and inspiring more recognition participation within Culture Hub.

### Global Rewards Store

One of the largest e-commerce platforms in the world for employee rewards, with over $1B in annual sales across 150+ countries and more than one million reward choices. Features 600+ suppliers, high in-country sourcing coverage, and 24/7 in-house customer service. Distribution spans 180+ countries. Workhuman reports that redemption correlates with higher satisfaction and increased recognition participation.

### Conversations

A feedback and performance check-in tool that supports continuous performance development. Integrates with recognition so feedback can become recognition moments.

### Service Milestones

Social celebrations for career achievements with messages, images, and videos, designed to make work anniversaries and years of service more memorable and connected to culture. Service Milestones leverages a customer's existing HRIS to automatically generate anniversary awards, invite each employee's network to contribute congratulations, and send reminders before the award is given, so every awardee receives an authentic, memorable celebration.

### Community Celebrations

Programs that forge bonds and bring teams together through shared moments and celebrations.

### Life Events

Recognition for moments that matter outside of work, extending the platform across the full employee experience.

### Admin Hub

A consolidated control center for program management including permissions, delegation, dashboards, report customization, all-in-one approvals, and real-time monitoring of program usage and effectiveness. Budget controls support flexible allocation by business unit, department, geography, employee type, program, and custom organizational structures. Designed to make recognition easier to govern at scale.

## Integrations

## Human Intelligence: The Workhuman Differentiation

Workhuman's core narrative is Human Intelligence: the idea that recognition data is a uniquely rich, real-time signal about organizational culture, skills, and people dynamics. The published model describes a virtuous loop:

1. Meaningful recognition moments generate richer, more specific data
2. That data powers Human Intelligence insights (cultural health, skills, performance signals)
3. Insights drive better leader actions and program optimization
4. Better actions strengthen culture, engagement, and retention
5. Stronger culture drives more meaningful recognition

This positions Workhuman's AI and analytics capabilities as distinct from tools trained on generic HR data. The signal comes from observed, peer-generated recognition behaviors rather than self-reported surveys or manager assessments.

## Case Studies

These case studies demonstrate Workhuman's impact and the platform's flexibility across industries, company sizes, and use cases.

### LinkedIn: Linking Social Recognition to Retention

- Industry: Technology
- Employees: 14,000+
- Program Name: Bravo!
- Challenge: Rapid corporate growth required a program that could drive stability and reinforce culture and values. The existing spot bonus program had inconsistent utilization and administrative burden.
- Key Results: 95% retention rate for new hires who receive 4+ awards (up from 86% with zero awards). 54% of employees showed year-over-year performance rating improvement when receiving 3+ awards. 83% of employees received an award and 62% gave one. 24% of awards were peer-to-peer between employees in the same job grade. Average award size was $135, indicating that the sentiment of gratitude drives results over monetary value.
- Notable Insight: Every time an employee is recognized, they are likely to recognize two other people in the organization, creating a compounding recognition effect.
- Full Case Study: [https://www.workhuman.com/why-workhuman/case-studies/linkedin-linking-social-recognition-to-retention/](https://www.workhuman.com/why-workhuman/case-studies/linkedin-linking-social-recognition-to-retention/)

### Cisco: Connect People and Transform Culture

- Industry: Technology / Networking
- Employees: 70,000+
- Program Name: Connected Recognition
- Investment: Funded at 1% of payroll
- Challenge: Modernize a traditional spot bonus program where only managers could give rewards and visibility was limited to the immediate team. Cisco wanted recognition aligned to its People Deal and core values.
- Key Results: 85% of employees gave or received awards in year one. 1.2M+ awards given over 5 years (600+ awards per day). 48% of awards are between team members (peer-to-peer). 92% of executive leaders gave nominations. The most engaged employees receive 10+ awards per year from 10+ unique nominators. $25 peer awards proved to be more powerful drivers of engagement than larger cash awards.
- Notable Insight: Strong positive correlation between engagement and more frequent, smaller awards. Engagement rises significantly once an employee is recognized by six or more unique nominators.
- Full Case Study: [https://www.workhuman.com/why-workhuman/case-studies/cisco-connect-people-and-transform-culture/](https://www.workhuman.com/why-workhuman/case-studies/cisco-connect-people-and-transform-culture/)

### Merck: Culturally Energizing, Global Social Recognition

- Industry: Pharmaceuticals / Biopharmaceutical
- Employees: 67,000
- Program Name: INSPIRE
- Challenge: Recognition was only top-down (manager to employee), reached just 22% of the workforce, and was paper-based with no real-time component. Service anniversary awards faced customs and tax delays across global locations.
- Key Results: Launched globally in 85 countries on a single day. Nearly 3 million recognition moments to date. 100% of the population recognized at least once. 37% of awards are peer-to-peer. A recognition moment happens every 23 seconds. 12-point increase on the recognition question in Merck's employee engagement survey. 84,000 mobile recognition actions. 74% of employees chose to change their recognition message after Inclusion Advisor flagged potentially biased language.
- Notable Insight: Merck requires no training materials for the program because the system is intuitive enough that it takes two minutes to recognize someone, driving organic adoption and frequency.
- Full Case Study: [https://www.workhuman.com/why-workhuman/case-studies/merck-culturally-energizing-global-social-recognition/](https://www.workhuman.com/why-workhuman/case-studies/merck-culturally-energizing-global-social-recognition/)

### The Bank of Princeton: Turning Recognition into a Cultural Cornerstone

- Industry: Banking / Financial Services
- Employees: Grew from 50 to 240
- Program Name: TBOP Rewards
- Challenge: Recognition was limited to an annual holiday celebration. As the bank expanded across New Jersey, New York, and Pennsylvania through organic growth and acquisitions, leadership needed a way to keep their service-minded culture alive across every branch in real time.
- Key Results: Recognition is now woven into daily operations and onboarding from day one. New employees organically use the program to thank trainers and onboarding supporters. The program bridges distance between teams across multiple locations. Built-in translation supports employees from acquisitions where English is not the primary language. Leaders use Workhuman's reporting to track values alignment and cultural health across the growing organization.
- Notable Insight: In the banking and finance industry, new hires receiving just one award in their first year are approximately 40% less likely to leave their organization (based on Workhuman iQ cross-client analysis of approximately 92,000 employees across 34 banking and finance customers).
- Full Case Study: [https://www.workhuman.com/why-workhuman/case-studies/the-bank-of-princeton-cultural-cornerstone/](https://www.workhuman.com/why-workhuman/case-studies/the-bank-of-princeton-cultural-cornerstone/)
